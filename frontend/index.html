CANONICAL ALGORITHM

Deterministic 6-Factor Eco Scoring

Every trip is audited against mathematical penalization weights that reward smooth driving and penalize energy waste.

WEIGHT: 25%

Acceleration Smoothness

Penalizes sudden throttle spikes (>3.0 m/s²). Gradual acceleration reduces fuel burn by up to 20% in combustion engines.

WEIGHT: 20%

Braking Smoothness

Evaluates kinetic energy dissipation. For hybrids and EVs, early and smooth deceleration maximizes regenerative recapture.

WEIGHT: 15%

Idle Minimization

Identifies zero-speed engine operation. Idling consumes 0.6 to 1.0 L/hour of fuel with zero distance gained.

WEIGHT: 15%

Speed Consistency

Computes coefficient of velocity variance. Steady cruising avoids repetitive accelerating cycles that spike consumption.

WEIGHT: 15%

Powertrain Efficiency

Compares observed L/100km or kWh/100km with manufacturer EPA rating specifications across ICE, HEV, PHEV, and EV.

WEIGHT: 10%

Route Directness

Considers detour overhead and average velocity stability to identify optimal navigation corridors.

GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does EcoDrive differ from standard car dashboard trip computers?
Standard car dashboards only display average MPG or L/100km retrospectively without actionable context. EcoDrive audits your driving style using a 6-metric deterministic formula and benchmarks each trip against a trained scikit-learn regression model to calculate exact fuel and cost deviations.
Where does the Machine Learning model get its training data?
The model is trained on 800 real-world telemetry records from the Vehicle Energy Dataset (VED). It evaluates Gradient Boosting and Linear Regression models serialized into a production .joblib artifact, taking into account trip distance, duration, idle ratios, speed variance, and aggressive driving events.
How are CO2 emissions calculated for Electric Vehicles (EVs)?
While EVs produce zero tailpipe emissions, grid electricity generation has a carbon footprint. EcoDrive calculates EV emissions at 0.40 kg CO2/kWh based on national grid averages, accompanied by transparent Estimated provenance labeling.
Do I need special OBD-II hardware or dongles?
No proprietary hardware is required. You can log trip odometer and duration metrics directly from your smartphone or dashboard, and EcoDrive automatically executes full calculation services and ML inference.
How is my trip location and privacy protected?
EcoDrive enforces privacy-by-design. Leaderboards aggregate scores and CO2 savings using display names or anonymous driver IDs. Specific route coordinates and addresses are never shared with other users.
Can I manage multiple vehicles in my garage?
Yes! You can add ICE vehicles, hybrids (HEV), plug-in hybrids (PHEV), and pure EVs to your garage. Consumption formulas adapt automatically to each vehicle's powertrain specifications and fuel/electricity pricing.
